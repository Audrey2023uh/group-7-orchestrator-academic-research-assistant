Interactive multi-agent research workflow with persistent memory

This Academic Research Assistant helps you study a paper the way a research team would: it reads the work, gathers several independent reviews, checks that the results are complete, and writes a short overall summary. You can save important findings and come back later without starting over.

Launch Live App

What it does

Load a paper, ask a research question, and let several specialist reviewers examine it independently before a combined report is produced.

It remembers your work

Your sessions, reviewed papers, key findings, and decisions are saved so you can continue later or look back at earlier results.

You stay in control

Pin a finding, approve a decision, delete one saved item, or clear all memory when you no longer need it.

How to use it

  1. Click Launch Live App to open the interactive assistant.
  2. Type your research question or notes (for example, “focus on limitations”).
  3. Keep the bundled paper, or paste your own paper text.
  4. Choose how many reviewers to run, then click Launch workflow.
  5. Watch the agents work, then read the reviews and the final summary.
  6. Save anything important in memory, or delete it if you want a clean start.
