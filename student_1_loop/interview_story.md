# Interview Story — Infinite Loop Guardrail

During integration testing of our academic research orchestrator, the coordinator kept re-dispatching rejected validation cycles without a hard stop. In an unguarded simulation the retry loop burned 100 iterations and about 12,000 approximate tokens before a safety hard-cap halted the run. After implementing deterministic `round_number` tracking, any retry storm that reaches `round_number >= 5` routes to a graceful partial-output node instead of continuing. With the guardrail enabled, the same perpetual-rejection scenario terminates in six coordinator steps at round five, cutting token spend to a few hundred units and collapsing latency relative to the unguarded path. The quantitative before/after comparison is recorded in `metrics.md` and reproduced by `test_failure.py`, giving a concrete production control for runaway graph recursion without relying on prompt instructions alone.
